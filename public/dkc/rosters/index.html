Rounds come from the 2025 DKC draft on Yahoo. Anyone absent from that draft was a waiver add and is marked FA, which counts as round 14.

The “kept ’25” marks are reconstructed, not copied. Yahoo does not retain past keeper declarations, so each 2025 draft slot was matched against the prior year’s roster and cost. It resolves cleanly for all ten teams, but Prashant should confirm it before anyone declares.

Traded players are priced at the round they were drafted, whoever drafted them, so a player acquired mid-season carries his original cost.

Why a lock can follow a trade. The 2017 sheet settles it: Isaiah Crowell, Rob Gronkowski, and DeMarco Murray were each kept, then traded, and each was still marked “cannot franchise” on his new team. Three cases, no exceptions, so the restriction attaches to the player rather than the manager.
